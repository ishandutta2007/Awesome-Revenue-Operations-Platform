## Top Revenue Operations Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on Revenue Intelligence, Pipeline Forecasting, Lead Routing, CRM Hygiene, Activity Capture, GTM Alignment & RevOps Automation*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Revenue Operations (RevOps)**. These tools help align marketing, sales, and customer success by improving forecasting accuracy, pipeline visibility, data quality, lead-to-account matching, activity capture, and revenue process automation.



**Examples** include Clari, BoostUp, Revenue Grid, People.ai, Syncari, Openprise, LeanData, Fullcast, RevSure, InsightSquared, Scratchpad, Aviso, HubSpot Operations Hub, Salesforce Revenue Intelligence, and related platforms (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise revenue intelligence and forecasting platforms remain largely commercial. Open-source strength lies in CRM foundations (**Twenty**, **Odoo**, **SuiteCRM**), workflow automation (**n8n**), data integration (**Airbyte**, **dbt**), analytics (**Metabase**, **Superset**), and emerging RevOps/AI pipeline projects. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms



- **[Clari](https://www.clari.com/)**  

  Leading enterprise revenue platform for pipeline inspection, forecasting, and revenue orchestration (now expanded through broader sales engagement capabilities).



- **[BoostUp](https://www.boostup.ai/)**  

  AI-powered pipeline management and forecasting platform popular with mid-market teams for deal inspection and forecast accuracy.



- **[People.ai](https://www.people.ai/)**  

  Revenue intelligence platform that automatically captures sales activities from email, calendar, and calls and maps them into the CRM for better visibility and coaching.



- **[LeanData](https://www.leandata.com/)**  

  Salesforce-native lead-to-account matching, routing, and revenue orchestration platform widely used for complex B2B lead management.



- **[Aviso / Aviso AI](https://www.aviso.com/)**  

  AI-native forecasting and revenue intelligence platform focused on predictive deal scoring and forecast reliability.



- **[Syncari, Openprise](https://www.syncari.com/)**  

  Data unification and revenue data operations platforms that keep CRM, marketing, and customer data synchronized and clean.



- **[Fullcast](https://www.fullcast.com/)**  

  Go-to-market planning and territory/quota management platform that supports capacity planning and GTM policy enforcement.



- **[Scratchpad](https://www.scratchpad.com/)**  

  CRM productivity and hygiene tool that helps sales reps update Salesforce faster and keeps pipeline data accurate.



- **[HubSpot Operations Hub & Salesforce Revenue Intelligence](https://www.hubspot.com/products/operations)**  

  Native RevOps and revenue intelligence capabilities embedded in major CRM ecosystems for mid-market and enterprise teams.



- **[Revenue Grid, RevSure, InsightSquared and others](https://www.revenuegrid.com/)**  

  Additional revenue intelligence, forecasting, and analytics platforms serving various segments of the RevOps stack.



## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[Twenty](https://github.com/twentyhq/twenty)**  

  Modern open-source CRM designed as a Salesforce alternative, with strong pipeline management, customization, and growing AI capabilities.



- **[Odoo / ERPNext CRM & Sales modules](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  

  Full open-source ERP suites that include robust CRM, pipeline, quotation, and revenue-tracking modules suitable for end-to-end RevOps foundations.



- **[SuiteCRM / EspoCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)**  

  Established open-source CRM platforms that support sales pipelines, forecasting views, and custom RevOps workflows.



- **[Relaticle](https://github.com/relaticle/relaticle)**  

  Open-source CRM with native AI agent support, MCP tools, and modern Laravel/Filament stack for self-hosted revenue operations.



- **[n8n](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)**  

  Open-source workflow automation platform frequently used to build custom RevOps pipelines (CRM sync, alerting, enrichment, and AI analysis).



- **[Airbyte + dbt](https://github.com/airbytehq/airbyte)**  

  Open-source data integration (Airbyte) and transformation (dbt) stack that powers many modern RevOps data platforms and reverse-ETL workflows.



- **[Metabase / Apache Superset](https://github.com/metabase/metabase)**  

  Open-source business intelligence tools commonly used for pipeline dashboards, forecast reporting, and revenue analytics.



- **[Custom RevOps & lead-to-cash projects](https://github.com/search?q=RevOps+OR+lead-to-cash+OR+revenue+intelligence+open+source)**  

  Emerging open-source engines for deal risk scoring, activity capture, pipeline automation, and GTM intelligence (including n8n-based and data-warehouse-centric examples).



- **[Other CRM & sales pipeline tools](https://github.com/search?q=open+source+CRM+OR+sales+pipeline)**  

  Additional community CRM, Kanban pipeline, and forecasting prototypes that can serve as building blocks.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Workflow & orchestration**: Temporal, Prefect, or Dagster for reliable RevOps data pipelines.

- **Identity resolution & enrichment helpers**: Open libraries and scripts for lead-to-account matching logic.

- **Activity capture experiments**: Community tools that parse email/calendar data into CRM records.

- **Forecasting models**: Python notebooks and libraries (Prophet, scikit-learn, etc.) for custom pipeline forecasting.

- **Reverse ETL**: Open-source options such as Grouparoo successors or custom dbt + warehouse patterns.

- Knowledge-base and Obsidian-style GTM systems of record adapted for RevOps documentation.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Start with an open-source CRM (**Twenty**, **Odoo**, or **SuiteCRM**) as the system of record.  

Layer **n8n** or similar for automation, **Airbyte + dbt** for data unification, and **Metabase/Superset** for dashboards and forecasting views.  

Commercial platforms (Clari, People.ai, LeanData, BoostUp, Aviso, etc.) excel at enterprise-grade forecasting accuracy, automatic activity capture, sophisticated lead routing, and AI-driven deal intelligence that most open-source stacks still require significant engineering effort to replicate.  

Many teams run a hybrid model: open-source CRM + data stack for core operations, with specialized commercial RevOps tools for forecasting and advanced intelligence.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.
